Find the reviewed (Swiss-Prot) UniProtKB entry for the human gene
MAOB. Save `target.csv` to your project workspace with the columns
`gene,accession,entry_name,sequence_length,residue_397` and exactly
one data row:
  - `accession`: the primary accession.
  - `entry_name`: the UniProt entry name, for example XXXX_HUMAN.
  - `sequence_length`: the length of the canonical sequence.
  - `residue_397`: the one-letter code of residue 397 of the
    canonical sequence, counting from 1.
