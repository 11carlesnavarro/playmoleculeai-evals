In PDB 6OIM, the bound inhibitor is covalently attached to the
protein. Identify the covalent linkage and characterise the
binding site. Save to your project workspace:
  - `covalent.csv`, one data row, columns
    `chain,resid,resname,protein_atom,ligand_atom,bond_length_A`:
    the protein residue covalently bonded to the inhibitor, the
    names of the two bonded atoms (protein and ligand), and the
    bond length in Å.
  - `pocket.csv` (columns `chain,resid,resname`): the protein
    residues with any heavy atom within 4 Å of the inhibitor.
