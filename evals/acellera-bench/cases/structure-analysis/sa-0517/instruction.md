PDB 2VT4 is an engineered crystallisation construct of a
drug-target receptor. Relative to the wild-type sequence of the
same protein from the same organism, the construct carries
deliberately introduced point mutations. Identify them. Save to
your project workspace `mutations.csv` (columns
`resid,wildtype,mutant`): one row per engineered single-residue
substitution, where `resid` is the residue number in the
wild-type (UniProt) numbering, and `wildtype` and `mutant` are
the one-letter amino acids before and after the substitution.
Exclude chain truncations, deletions, insertions, expression
tags, and any fusion-partner residues.
